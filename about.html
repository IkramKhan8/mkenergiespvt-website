Built for a fuel that demands discipline.

MK Energies (Private) Limited is a Pakistan-registered LPG marketing company, formed to supply liquefied petroleum gas to domestic, commercial and industrial customers.

Our story

Entering Pakistan’s LPG sector with a long-term plan.

We are entering the sector at a time when LPG plays a growing role in Pakistan’s energy mix, particularly in regions beyond the reach of the natural gas pipeline network.

Our approach is straightforward: source LPG responsibly, store and handle it to the standards the fuel requires, and move it through a distribution network that puts safety and consistency first.

Every part of our operation, from storage and filling to transportation and retail supply, is designed to be built and operated in line with the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) LPG Rules, 2001. The plan is clear: grow a compliant, safety-first LPG marketing network across Pakistan.

Our approach

Source, handle, deliver. No shortcuts.

Every kilogram of LPG we market moves through the same three stages, and each stage is held to its own standard.

  1. 01 / Source

    Source responsibly

    LPG from Pakistan’s domestic producers and refineries, and through imports, under proper supply arrangements.

  2. 02 / Handle

    Handle to standard

    Storage, filling and transport built to the standards the fuel requires, with pressure, quality and safety checks at every stage.

  3. 03 / Deliver

    Deliver consistently

    A dealer and distributor network that puts safety and consistency first, from the plant to your premises.
