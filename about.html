Built for work that demands discipline.

MK Energies (Private) Limited is an Islamabad-based energy and mining company. We explore, drill, mine and supply across oil and gas, LPG, petroleum and minerals.

Who we are

One company. Two divisions.

Pakistan’s demand for energy and minerals keeps growing. MK Energies was formed to meet it with discipline: safe operations, full compliance and dependable delivery.

Our energy division works in oil and gas exploration, drilling, LPG and gas distribution, and petroleum and energy services. Our mining division works in mineral exploration, mining and extraction, and mineral logistics.

Both divisions share one leadership team, one safety system and one point of contact, so clients and partners deal with a single accountable company.

How we work

Four commitments on every job.

The same principles guide a drilling programme, a mine site and a gas delivery.

  • Safety before schedule

    No job is so urgent that it can’t be done safely. Work stops when conditions aren’t right.

  • Compliance built in

    Every project runs under the licences, concessions and permits its regulator requires.

  • Strong technical partners

    We work with experienced geologists, drilling contractors and engineers on each project.

  • Delivery you can plan around

    Scope, schedule and pricing agreed before work starts, and one team to call.
