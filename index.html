How LPG reaches you

From source to doorstep, in four controlled stages.

LPG in Pakistan comes from domestic producers and refineries, and from imports. It reaches customers through a regulated marketing chain. This is where MK Energies operates.

  1. 01 Source

    Production & import

    LPG sourced from Pakistan’s domestic producers and refineries, and through imports, under market supply arrangements.

  2. 02 Store

    Storage & filling

    Received into storage and filling infrastructure built to OGRA requirements, with pressure, quality and safety checks at every stage.

  3. 03 Move

    Bowser transport

    Moved by registered road bowsers under documented handling procedures to distribution points across the network.

  4. 04 Deliver

    Dealer & doorstep

    Delivered to authorized dealers and distributors, and on to homes, businesses and industrial customers.

Safety & compliance

Built around OGRA’s rulebook, from day one.

Pakistan’s LPG sector is regulated by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority under the LPG (Production and Distribution) Rules, 2001. We structure our operations, infrastructure and documentation to meet them as we establish and grow.

Our safety commitments
  1. 01
    SECP incorporation

    Registered as a private limited company under Pakistani corporate law, the foundation OGRA requires before any licensing step.

  2. 02
    OGRA marketing license

    Application and site documentation prepared in line with OGRA’s checklist for LPG marketing licenses.

  3. 03
    Storage & filling plant standards

    Site selection, distance and zoning requirements, and third-party inspection built into facility planning.

  4. 04
    Transport registration

    Road bowsers operated only through OGRA-registered vehicles and trained drivers.

  5. 05
    Authorized distributor network

    Dealers and distributors onboarded under formal agreements consistent with OGRA’s distribution framework.
