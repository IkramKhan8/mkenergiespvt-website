About Us

A new LPG marketing company, built on old-fashioned discipline.

MK Energies (Private) Limited is a Pakistan-registered LPG marketing company formed to supply liquefied petroleum gas to domestic, commercial and industrial customers. We are entering the sector at a time when LPG plays a growing role in Pakistan's energy mix, particularly in regions beyond the reach of the natural gas pipeline network.

Our approach is straightforward: source LPG responsibly, store and handle it to the standards the fuel requires, and move it through a distribution network that puts safety and consistency first. Every part of our operation — from storage and filling to transportation and retail supply — is designed to be built and operated in line with the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA)'s LPG Rules, 2001.

The company is led by two directors who bring complementary operational and business perspectives to the venture, and is headquartered with a clear long-term plan: grow a compliant, safety-first LPG marketing network across Pakistan.

Regulatory Commitment

Built around OGRA's rulebook, from day one.

The LPG sector in Pakistan is regulated by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority under the LPG (Production and Distribution) Rules, 2001. MK Energies is structuring its operations, infrastructure and documentation to meet these requirements as it establishes and grows.

MK Energies (Private) Limited is a newly formed company. Licensing, storage-plant approvals and distributor authorizations are pursued and maintained through OGRA's formal application process; this page describes our compliance commitment, not a claim of licenses already granted.
  • SECP IncorporationRegistered as a private limited company under Pakistani corporate law, the foundation OGRA requires before any licensing step.
  • OGRA Marketing LicenseApplication and site documentation prepared in line with OGRA's checklist for LPG marketing licenses.
  • Storage & Filling Plant StandardsSite selection, distance and zoning requirements, and third-party inspection built into our facility planning.
  • Transport RegistrationRoad bowsers operated only through OGRA-registered vehicles and trained drivers.
  • Authorized Distributor NetworkDealers and distributors onboarded under formal agreements consistent with OGRA's distribution framework.
