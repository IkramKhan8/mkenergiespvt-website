How we work

From survey to supply.

We cover the whole chain, so a project moves from first study to delivered product without changing hands.

  1. Step 01

    Explore

    Geological studies, surveys and sampling to find and prove oil, gas and mineral resources.

  2. Step 02

    Drill

    Exploration and development drilling with certified rigs and trained crews.

  3. Step 03

    Extract & produce

    Mining, production and site work run to strict health, safety and environment standards.

  4. Step 04

    Deliver

    Storage, transport and distribution of gas, petroleum and minerals to customers.

Health, safety & environment

Zero harm is the only target.

Exploration, drilling and mining work carries real risk. Every project starts with a risk assessment and runs under the licences, concessions and permits its regulator requires.

How we work safely

  • Risk assessed first

    Every job starts with a documented risk assessment and a safe method of work.

  • Certified equipment

    Rigs, vehicles, storage and site equipment inspected and maintained to standard.

  • Trained crews

    Staff, drivers and contractors trained and briefed for the work they do.

  • Environment protected

    Spill prevention, waste handling and site restoration planned before work starts.
