General 04

MK Energies (Private) Limited is an LPG marketing company. We are building capability across the LPG value chain: cylinder distribution, bulk and industrial supply, storage and filling, road bowser transport, and an authorized dealer and distributor network.

Our head office is at Office No. 8, 1st Floor, Al Jannat Plaza, Markaz G-11, Islamabad. We are building a supply and dealer network to serve customers across Pakistan.

Supply & pricing 04

Households, commercial users such as restaurants and hotels, and industrial customers. Homes and small businesses buy through our authorized dealers, and larger users can arrange bulk supply directly with our sales team.

Yes. Bulk and industrial supply is one of our core service lines: scheduled bulk deliveries for industrial, commercial and hospitality customers, with storage and offtake planning for your site. Request a bulk supply quote.

OGRA announces the consumer price of LPG every month, per kilogram and for the standard 11.8 kg domestic cylinder. For bulk and industrial supply, our sales team quotes based on volume and delivery location.

The standard domestic cylinder holds 11.8 kg of LPG. Commercial users commonly use larger 45.4 kg cylinders, and high-volume sites can move to bulk storage. Our sales team can advise on the right format for your use.

Safety 03

Yes, when it is stored and used correctly: cylinders upright in a ventilated place, regulators and hoses in good condition, and gas bought only from an authorized dealer. LPG has an added odour so that leaks can be noticed.

Don’t touch electrical switches, put out any flames, close the cylinder valve if it is safe to do so, and open doors and windows. Then leave the area and call your LPG supplier or Rescue 1122 from a safe distance. Read our safety guidance.
