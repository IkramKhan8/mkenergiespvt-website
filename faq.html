LPG supply 4

Households, commercial users such as restaurants and hotels, and industrial customers. Homes and small businesses buy through our authorized dealers, and larger users can arrange bulk supply directly with our sales team.

OGRA announces the consumer price of LPG every month, per kilogram and for the standard 11.8 kg domestic cylinder. For bulk and industrial supply, our sales team quotes based on volume and delivery location.

The standard domestic cylinder holds 11.8 kg of LPG. Commercial users commonly use larger 45.4 kg cylinders, and high-volume sites can move to bulk storage. Our sales team can advise on the right format for your use.

Call our sales team on +92 345 5935190, or send an enquiry and choose “Becoming a distributor or dealer”. We onboard dealers and distributors under formal agreements consistent with OGRA’s distribution framework.
