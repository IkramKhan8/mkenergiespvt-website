How LPG reaches you

From source to doorstep, in four controlled stages.

LPG in Pakistan comes from domestic producers and refineries, and from imports. It reaches customers through a regulated marketing chain. This is where MK Energies operates.

  1. 01 Source

    Production & import

    LPG sourced from Pakistan’s domestic producers and refineries, and through imports, under market supply arrangements.

  2. 02 Store

    Storage & filling

    Received into storage and filling infrastructure built to OGRA requirements, with pressure, quality and safety checks at every stage.

  3. 03 Move

    Bowser transport

    Moved by registered road bowsers under documented handling procedures to distribution points across the network.

  4. 04 Deliver

    Dealer & doorstep

    Delivered to authorized dealers and distributors, and on to homes, businesses and industrial customers.
